# 2-Minute Submission Demo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 120 Seconds (2:00)  
**Presenter:** Engineering Team  
**Focus:** Brand Aesthetic, Truthful PAP Model, Interactive Track Finder, Client Sprints, and Accessibility Rigor.

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] Hook & Brand Identity (Desktop 1440px)
* **Visual:** Start on the Home Page hero section (`https://karka.academy`). Slow smooth pan over the Fraunces display typography, the Tamil hook badge (`கற்க • Learn First • Pay When Hired`), and the dynamic 3D Orbit Canvas.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Welcome to our redesign of Karka AI & Tech Academy. Traditional tech bootcamps in Tier-2 Tamil Nadu often rely on outdated templates, false promises, and heavy upfront loans. We rebuilt Karka from the ground up as a modern, Gen-Z-friendly engineering hub that reflects their vibrant Instagram presence `@karka.ai` while enforcing absolute truth in advertising."*
* **Action:** Hover over the 3D Orbit nodes showing Full-Stack, Generative AI, and Data Science orbiting the central core.

---

### [0:20 – 0:45] The Pay After Placement Model (Desktop / Tablet)
* **Visual:** Scroll down to the Proof Bar and Pay After Placement section (`#pap-model`). Show the 3-step timeline and side-by-side comparison table.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Karka’s core differentiator is its Pay After Placement model. Notice our radical transparency: learners pay ₹0 upfront tuition. A single flat fee of ₹40,000 is due only after accepting an official software job offer. Unlike predatory 20% salary ISAs or high-interest NBFC loans, Karka shares the risk completely."*
* **Action:** Highlight the ₹0 vs ₹80k comparison card and click to open the contract terms breakdown.

---

### [0:45 – 1:10] Interactive Career Quiz & Course Catalog
* **Visual:** Scroll to the embedded Track Finder Quiz.
* **Narration:**  
  *"To eliminate confusion for beginners, we built an interactive Career Track Finder. With just 4 quick questions about current status, interests, coding comfort, and goals..."*
* **Action:** Click through: *Recent Graduate → Building Web Apps → Total Beginner → Land First Job*.
* **Visual:** The recommendation card animates: *Full-Stack Development (PAP Eligible)* with starter courses.
* **Action:** Click *"Browse All 37 Courses"*. Type *"React"* in the search bar. Show instant instant client-side filtering down to relevant cards. Click *"MERN Full Stack"* to reveal the multi-stage syllabus accordion.

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] Real Client Sprints & Verified Placements
* **Visual:** Navigate to `/internships` and `/placements`.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Students don't build generic To-Do apps. Through Agile Tribers, Karka learners ship code for real fintech and enterprise clients—like the Flow Global lending portal. On our placements page, we feature verified alumnus Gabriel Samraj who cracked Capecom Solutions, and in compliance with competition ethics, we display hiring partners like Amazon, TCS, and HCL as clean text badges with zero third-party logos."*
* **Action:** Click the video thumbnail to preview the student testimonial modal.

---

### [1:35 – 2:00] Mobile Experience & Engineering Rigor (Mobile 390px)
* **Visual:** Switch browser DevTools to Mobile viewport (390×844px). Show the sticky bottom action bar with 1-tap WhatsApp and Apply buttons.
* **Narration:**  
  *"Everything is engineered mobile-first for mid-range Android on 4G. On low-end devices or with reduced motion, our capability detector drops the 3D canvas for a zero-CLS poster. All 12 color token pairs exceed WCAG 2.2 AA contrast standards, and our full pipeline—from strict CSP to unit tests and 60 static pages—passes all automated quality gates with zero errors. Thank you!"*
* **Action:** Tap the WhatsApp button to reveal the prefilled mentor URL, then tap *"Apply"* to show the responsive dialog with client validation.
