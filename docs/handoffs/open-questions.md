# Open Questions & Documented Assumptions

This document tracks questions for Karka AI & Tech Academy stakeholders, along with the reasonable working assumptions taken by the engineering team to avoid project stalls.

---

## 1. Academy Confirmation Questions & Documented Assumptions

### Q1: Pay After Placement (PAP) Terms & Structure
- **Question:** Does the ₹40,000 fee (from the Roadmap page) apply universally to all tracks? What is the minimum salary threshold, payment period (e.g. 12 months), and instalment schedule? What happens if a student is not placed?
- **Working Assumption:** We state the transparent terms at a glance: ₹0 upfront tuition, pay ₹40,000 only after placement with an official employment offer. Exact salary thresholds and payment schedules are flagged as `todo` and will display as "Terms detailed in admission agreement" without inventing numbers.

### Q2: Placement Statistics & Student Stories
- **Question:** Can the academy provide verified counts of learners trained and placed, along with 6–10 real learner profiles (name, role, company, quote, photo permission)?
- **Working Assumption:** We do NOT claim "100% placement" or "24 LPA highest package" anywhere on the redesign. Gabriel Samraj is featured with his verified video and quote; other placement cards focus on the 5-step placement preparation process and live internship experience at Agile Tribers rather than fake student profiles.

### Q3: Hiring Partner Logos vs Names
- **Question:** Does Karka possess commercial rights and written permission to display third-party corporate logos (Amazon, TCS, HCL, Matrimony.com, Novac, Capecom, Flow, Sodisys)?
- **Working Assumption:** To strictly comply with competition originality and trademark rules, we display hiring partners as cleanly styled typographic text pills rather than copyrighted SVG/PNG corporate logos.

### Q4: Mentors & Instructors
- **Question:** Who are the lead instructors for each course track to replace the template placeholders (John Doe, Sarah Connor, etc.)?
- **Working Assumption:** We highlight the verified founder & chief mentor Sateesh Kumar (18+ years, Ex-CTO Flow Global, Wipro, Yatra) and showcase the institutional mentorship model ("Industry Veterans & On-Site Practitioners") without hardcoding unverified individual instructor cards.

### Q5: Brand Assets & Visual Identity
- **Question:** Are high-resolution vectors of the Karka logo and the AI Robot Mascot approved for use in the redesign?
- **Working Assumption:** We use our own team-crafted SVG logo and generative high-performance WebP mascot imagery inspired by `@karka.ai` Instagram aesthetic, documented in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.

### Q6: Tamil Regional Hook
- **Question:** Does the academy endorse featuring the Tamil script root "கற்க" (*Karka* = *to learn*) in the hero and brand narrative?
- **Working Assumption:** Yes, a subtle, tasteful "கற்க" flourish will be integrated into the hero title and footer to give authentic regional pride to the Nagercoil center.

### Q7: Operating Hours & Exact Address
- **Question:** What is the precise street address, postal code, and visiting hours for the Nagercoil campus?
- **Working Assumption:** We display "Nagercoil, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu" with direct Google Maps geo-coordinates (`8.1801487, 77.4311084`), WhatsApp button, and contact form, marking specific street numbers and hours as `todo`.
